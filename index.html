How to Choose the Right Prompting Technique

Choosing the right prompting technique depends on your specific task and goals. Here's a guide to help you decide:

For Beginners (Start Here)

  • Zero-Shot Prompting : The simplest approach - just ask directly. Good for straightforward questions.
  • Few-Shot Prompting : Provide examples of what you want. Great when you need a specific format or style.
  • Role/Persona Prompting : Tell the AI to act as an expert in a field. Useful for specialized knowledge.

Choose by Task Type

For Complex Reasoning & Problem-Solving
  • Chain-of-Thought : When you need step-by-step reasoning (math, logic problems)
  • Tree-of-Thought : For problems with multiple possible approaches
  • Least-to-Most : Breaking complex problems into simpler sub-problems
For Accuracy & Verification
  • Chain-of-Verification : When accuracy is critical and you want the AI to verify its work
  • Chain-of-Evidence : When you need citations and evidence for claims
  • Self-Consistency : For getting the most reliable answer through multiple attempts
For Content Creation & Refinement
  • Self-Refinement : For iteratively improving content quality
  • Document Editing Mode : When editing or improving existing text
  • Format Structure : When you need a specific output format
For Specialized Tasks
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) : When you need up-to-date or specialized information
  • Document Analysis : For summarizing or analyzing documents
  • UI Mockup : For generating interface designs

Quick Decision Guide

Ask yourself these questions:

  1. Is my task straightforward or complex? (Straightforward → Zero-Shot, Complex → Chain-of-Thought)
  2. Do I need step-by-step reasoning? (Yes → Chain-of-Thought or Least-to-Most)
  3. Is factual accuracy critical? (Yes → Chain-of-Verification or Chain-of-Evidence)
  4. Am I creating or refining content? (Creating → Role/Persona, Refining → Self-Refinement)
  5. Do I need a specific format or structure? (Yes → Format Structure Prompting)
